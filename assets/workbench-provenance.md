- **Solidrift:** the original two-solid input and recorded FLOAT output show a missing body. New native computation requires local Blender.

Fresh-worker memory restoration is existing workbench behavior described in the README. QOI is by Dominic Szablewski and contributors; the emulator is Unicorn.js/Unicorn.
- **Caustryn:** the [recorded exact-counterfeit case](https://github.com/nazeeh111/Caustryn/blob/eae4af97547eb2f3c375496950256489fbbcfcc0/docs/demo/exact-counterfeit.json) supplies the fit, generator point, poses and diagnostics. Five training poses and two held poses retain the frozen fit. The stored coordinates yield a 3D separation of 3.4883707701485984 m; the picture plots their X/Y coordinates only. Reduced residuals are 0.9200242681745782 (training) and 1.0671951690878683 (held). This is a synthetic recorded case, not a fresh solver run or hardware measurement. Pose-dependent response produces the counterfeit; passing the held check does not establish stable response.
