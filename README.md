## From model to evidence

### [PhotonSmith](https://nazeeh111.github.io/work/photonsmith/) · Light through a designed structure

A periodic photonic grating, designed and checked with a Maxwell solver. The figure shows the retained electric field and spectrum from the numerical design, not a fabricated-device measurement.

<picture><source media="(max-width: 600px)" srcset="assets/profile-v3/photonsmith-mobile.png"><img src="assets/profile-v3/photonsmith.png" width="1000" alt="PhotonSmith's stored 1550-nanometre numerical design: real electric field in one periodic cell and target-order efficiency across wavelength, with axes and color scale."></picture>

### [Solidrift](https://nazeeh111.github.io/Solidrift/) · A union that lost a solid

A recorded Blender failure is reduced from 132 triangles to 12 while preserving the missing-body defect. The figure shows the original case; the linked walkthrough follows the reduction.

<picture><source media="(max-width: 600px)" srcset="assets/solidrift-comparison-mobile.svg"><img src="assets/solidrift-comparison.svg" width="1000" alt="The original Solidrift case: two overlapping mesh solids are input, but one body is absent from Blender's returned union. The later reduction reaches a 12-triangle witness."></picture>

## More work

**[SiliconPilot](https://nazeeh111.github.io/work/siliconpilot/)** compiles a small neural classifier to fixed-point Verilog; compare recorded FPGA designs and simulated latency. [Circuits](https://nazeeh111.github.io/SILICONPILOT/) · [Source](https://github.com/nazeeh111/SILICONPILOT)

**[MaterialMind](https://nazeeh111.github.io/work/materialmind/)** compares crystal-stability predictions with archived DFT. In the recorded PaMnF6 case, the reference falls outside the stated prediction interval. [Worked case](https://github.com/nazeeh111/MaterialMind/blob/c879afa4b616649cac73ef46b010f24e12ed3544/docs/presentation/README.md)

**[MorphProof](https://nazeeh111.github.io/work/morphproof/)** translates supported numerical Python to Rust and exposes a deliberately omitted loop with a one-element input. [Compiler and Studio](https://github.com/nazeeh111/MorphProof)

**[LadderProof](https://nazeeh111.github.io/work/ladderproof/)** finds where an ideal resistor-ladder DAC can step backward and verifies tolerance bounds in the circuit model. [Source](https://github.com/nazeeh111/LadderProof)
